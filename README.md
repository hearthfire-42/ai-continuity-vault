## What This Is

A starter vault for building real continuity with your AI collaborator. Drop these files into your Obsidian vault (or any folder your AI can read), and stop introducing yourself every morning.

This isn't theory. These templates come from a working system — the same structure used daily by the team behind Rooted Rhythms. They work with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any AI that reads project files.

## What's Inside

```
README.md              ← you're reading it
CLAUDE.md              ← your AI's orientation file (read every session)
WAKE.md                ← session start: where you left off, what's next
carry-forward.md       ← end-of-session notes template
memory/
  MEMORY.md            ← index of things worth remembering across sessions
  example-memory.md    ← what a memory entry looks like
.claude/
  rules/
    communication.md   ← how you want your AI to talk to you
```

## How to Use It

**Step 1:** Copy this folder into your project directory (or Obsidian vault).

**Step 2:** Edit `CLAUDE.md` — fill in who you are, what you're working on, how you like to communicate. This is the file your AI reads first, every time. Make it yours.

**Step 3:** Before your next AI session, fill in `WAKE.md` with what you worked on last time. Even three bullet points is enough. Your AI reads this and picks up where you left off instead of asking "How can I help you today?"

**Step 4:** At the end of each session, spend 60 seconds updating `carry-forward.md`. What you did, what's next, anything that surprised you. Tomorrow-you will thank today-you.

**Step 5:** When something is worth remembering across weeks (not just tomorrow), add it to `memory/`. A preference, a decision pattern, a lesson learned. Your AI reads this index and carries it forward.

## Why Markdown?

Your AI's native language isn't English. It's markdown.

`.md` files are plain text with lightweight formatting. They're human-readable, AI-readable, grep-able, version-controllable, and they never break. No proprietary format. No cloud dependency. No app lock-in.

When you write your AI a `.md` file, you're speaking its first language. Everything else is translation.

## The Philosophy

Most AI platforms wipe your context every session. They call it a fresh start. It's not a fresh start — it's amnesia.

You wouldn't hire an assistant who forgets your name every morning. You wouldn't collaborate with a coworker who loses yesterday's work every night. But that's what most AI tools ask you to accept as normal.

This vault is the alternative. It's simple, it's portable, and it puts continuity in YOUR hands — not the platform's.

The design can change. It starts with you.
